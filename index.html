Draft Rules

Roster Management & Trades

Waivers use FAAB with a $1,000 budget.

The Palmer Rule

Players ruled out one hour or earlier before kickoff may trigger the Palmer Rule penalty.

04

Strategic Benching

Managers may bench players when doing so guarantees a win.

05

Playoffs & Competitive Lineups

Managers must maintain competitive lineups when required.

06

The QB Limit Rule

Maximum of three active quarterbacks.

07

Mid-Week Waiver Bidding Rule

Special blind FAAB bidding may be used when needed.

08

The Damar Hamlin Rule

Special handling applies to canceled games or extraordinary events.

09

Season Format

Regular season Weeks 1–14. Playoffs Weeks 15–17.

10

Punishment for Last Place

Last-place punishment is selected by the league.

11

Commissioner Rules

A commissioner may be voted out by a 5/9 vote.

Point Changes
