Draft Rules Roster & Trades Palmer Rule Strategic Benching Playoffs QB Limit Mid-Week Waivers Damar Hamlin Rule Season Format Last Place Commissioner Rules Point Changes 01 Draft Rules Draft Type: Snake draft. The top three finishers from the previous season choose their draft positions. The remaining seven draft spots are assigned randomly. Roster: QB, QB, RB, RB, WR, WR, TE, FLEX (RB/WR/TE), FLEX (RB/WR/TE), DEF, 6 bench spots and 2 IR spots. Managers have 90 seconds per pick. If a manager misses a draft pick, that manager automatically goes to the end of the round. Beginning in 2027: no technology may be used during the draft. Paper only. A commissioner may use a computer if needed strictly for draft-keeping purposes. 02 Roster Management & Trades Players must be locked into the lineup before their individual game starts. Waivers use FAAB with a $1,000 budget. Teams may trade waiver spots and FAAB. Draft picks may only be traded before the draft. Future draft-pick trading is not allowed. No Loans: loans of players, FAAB or other assets are strictly prohibited. If the league court finds coaches guilty, the involved coaches receive a 20-point deduction the following week. Trades are not final until accepted in the app. Verbal or offline agreements are not binding. Trading is generally allowed at all times. During the playoffs, teams may not trade with teams that are out of the playoffs or with teams in the loser bracket. No collusion is allowed. Fannin Rule: waiver claims follow the fantasy app exactly. A manager must have the exact waiver claim needed for the transaction to process. The 2025 Fannin situation involving Zander is the precedent and everyone has been made aware of the rule. 03 The Palmer Rule If a player is ruled out one hour or earlier before kickoff and remains in a manager's starting lineup, that manager receives a 10-point deduction for the week. A manager may request an extenuating-circumstances vote to overturn the deduction. The affected manager and that manager's opponent for the week may not vote. A majority of 5 out of 8 votes from the remaining league members is required to overturn the penalty. The vote must take place before the following week's games. A manager who knows they may be unable to adjust a lineup because of travel, work or another issue may notify the commissioner before kickoff. The notification must identify the player in question and a backup player from the bench who has not yet played that week. 04 Strategic Benching Managers may bench players when doing so guarantees a weekly win. This applies to every position, including defense. For example, if a manager leads 152-150 with only a defense remaining on Monday night and the opponent has no players left, the manager may bench that defense to secure the victory. No deduction or penalty is applied for legitimate strategic benching. 05 Playoffs & Competitive Lineups When a team has an automatic bye, is automatically in the loser bracket, is mathematically eliminated, or otherwise has no position to gain or lose, that manager must start the best players available on the roster based on the highest projections. The starting lineup must be completely filled. Bye-week players and players ruled out may not occupy those required starting spots. If a commissioner determines that a violation is outrageous or malicious, the manager receives a 15-point deduction in the next matchup that matters. The penalty may roll into a later playoff round or even the following season. Examples in the rulebook include a team with a bye taking the deduction in Round 2, a seventh-place team taking it in the following season's Week 1, or a loser- bracket team taking it in the relevant punishment matchup. A team is considered mathematically eliminated if the required winning margin is greater than the largest weekly point differential in league history. The exact historical threshold is still to be finalized with Alex. Playoff trading is allowed, but playoff trades must be completed by the Thursday night game. 06 The QB Limit Rule A roster may have a maximum of three quarterbacks at a time, including starters and backups. A fourth quarterback is allowed if that player is in an IR spot or if two of the four quarterbacks are on bye. Once the exception no longer applies, the manager must return the roster to three quarterbacks by the start of that week's games. If the manager fails to drop a quarterback, the third-best quarterback according to official rankings or scoring is automatically dropped by the commissioner and made available on waivers. 07 Mid-Week Waiver Bidding Rule If a player remains on waivers but cannot be picked up because the official claim day falls on Wednesday after games have already been played, a coach may submit a bid through the commissioner's office before the next waiver period. The process remains a blind FAAB bid. All bids must be submitted before that player's game time. The highest bid wins. If bids are tied, standard waiver priority is the tiebreaker. The successful manager's dropped player must not have already played during the current NFL week. 08 The Damar Hamlin Rule In Loser Championship games during the playoffs, if a player dies on the field or a game is completely canceled and the matchup's winner or loser is not clear, this rule applies. If the outcome was obviously decided based on points, players remaining or other clear indicators, the punishment is assigned accordingly. If the result is genuinely uncertain, the punishment is divided between the two teams. 09 Season Format Regular Season: Weeks 1–14. Playoffs: Weeks 15–17. The top six teams qualify for the playoffs. The highest-seeded managers get to choose who they play in the playoffs. Tiebreakers: Total Points Scored → Head-to-Head Record → Points Against → Coin Flip if necessary. 10 Punishment for Last Place The coach who finishes last in the Loser's Bracket is subject to the punishment selected by the league before the season begins. If the punishment is not completed before the following season's draft, the coach loses draft picks as a consequence. 11 Commissioner Rules A commissioner may be voted out by a 5/9 vote. When the league needs a new commissioner, coaches may nominate themselves and the league then holds a vote. View Commissioner History → 12 Point Changes Forced fumble: +1 point. Fumble recovery: +1 point. Fumble: -1 point. Fumble lost: -1 point. These scoring rules apply to both D/ST and normal offensive players. Example: if Lamar fumbles and Derrick Henry recovers it, D/ST gets +1, Lamar gets -1 and Henry gets +1. If Lamar muffs a snap and recovers it himself, he receives -1 for the fumble and +1 for the recovery, resulting in a net zero. Defensive fourth-down stops receive +1 point. D/ST scoring is also adjusted for forcing and recovering muffed punts using the same fumble scoring framework above.
