Rulebook

The governing rules of Crippling Gambling Addicts.

League FormatQB Roster LimitPalmer RuleTradesWaiversRivalry WeekAwardsDamar Hamlin Rule
01

League Format

10-team, 2-QB, full-PPR fantasy football league. Established 2021 with a $200 annual buy-in.

02

QB Roster Limit

Maximum of three active quarterbacks. A fourth is allowed only while on IR. Once activated, the roster must return to three quarterbacks by game time.

03

Palmer Rule

If a player is ruled out within one hour before kickoff, the manager may be subject to a 10-point deduction unless an extenuating-circumstances vote passes. A manager can notify the commissioner before kickoff to request a swap to an eligible player who has not yet played.

04

Trades

A trade is not final until accepted in the league app. Player loans are prohibited. Only next-year draft picks may be traded.

05

Waiver Exception

When standard waiver timing would prevent a legitimate post-game pickup, a bid can be submitted to the commissioners. Highest valid bid wins; the dropped player cannot have already played.

06

Rivalry Week

Week 13 is reserved for designated league rivalries and carries its own place in CGA history.

07

Season Awards

$50 superlatives include highest weekly team score, highest single-player weekly score from a starter, most season points, and Steal of the Draft for an eligible late-round selection.

08

Damar Hamlin Rule

For loser-championship scenarios where a game is canceled or an extraordinary on-field event prevents a reasonable outcome, punishment can be split when the result is not otherwise obvious.
